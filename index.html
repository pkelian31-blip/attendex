▦ QR Session Mode
✓ Recommended for large classes (100–700+ students)
✓ One permanent QR projected on screen or printed
✓ Each student scans it once with their own phone
✓ Device fingerprint locks that phone — no one else can use it
✓ Matricule locks that student — cannot be submitted twice
✓ All 700 students can scan freely, at their own pace
✓ No timing pressure — QR stays valid for the session duration
🔄 Dynamic QR — How it works
• A new QR code appears every 60 seconds
• Each QR has a unique one-time token that expires with it
• Students who scan QR #1 are locked in — if QR #2 appears
  they just tap Submit, they don't need to re-scan
• Screenshots reused after expiry are rejected
• ⚠ Best for small classes (under 30) — with 700 students,
  use Static QR instead to avoid timing pressure
ℹ The QR rotation time below does NOT limit how long students
have to submit — it only controls how often the QR refreshes.
Students keep their token until they submit or the session ends.

How to use QR codes with attendance
Step 01
Create a session on the Dashboard. Copy the generated link.
Step 02
Come to the QR Tool tab, paste the link in the Session Link panel.
Step 03
Click Generate QR Code and download as PNG or SVG.
Step 04
Project or print the QR code — students scan and fill the form.
Step 05
When time is up, attendance auto-closes and PDF is generated.
Tip
Use SVG format for large prints — stays crisp at any scale.
